(§175): decision / source rule / alternatives / chosen / why / risk /
legal review required? / future consequence. Decisions are numbered
DR-1… and referenced from the other P5.6A documents. Where a decision
rests on a rule whose status is uncertain, the uncertainty register in
`M23_P56A_AGENT_REGULATORY_SNAPSHOT.md` §9 is the authority, not this
table.
