# M23 P8 — Defect register

Every defect the P8 audits and suites found, with the §88 fields. Severity
follows P6.1/P7/P7.1: Critical (fabricated or lost lifecycle / signing
truth), High (authorization, privacy, data integrity), Medium (correctness a
user or an auditor would notice; a dead end), Low (honesty, wording, UX,
documentation). Fixture and assertion mistakes are listed apart and are not
product defects.
