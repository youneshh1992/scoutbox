# M23 P5.7 — Defect register

Every temporal-integrity defect found by the sweep, with its severity,
reproduction, root cause, fix and regression. Severity follows the P5.6F
scale: **High** = a security, safeguarding or compliance decision fails open;
**Medium** = a lifecycle or product decision is wrong or a silently
coerced value is stored; **Low** = zone-dependent display, an unreachable
edge, or a fail-closed inconvenience. All are fixed unless stated; no fix
needed a migration.
